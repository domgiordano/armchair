# Design Research: Traitors visual language

**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Decision it informs**: the `traitors/` theme, type, tokens, FX and screen metaphors in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) (Frontend layout, PR 11, 12, 13, 15). The intro and loader in PR 15 build on it.
**Status**: Complete. The `unverified` items are listed under Open Questions.

## Question
What does the show actually look like? Which free fonts, colours, browser FX and CC0 assets let `traitors/` read as "gaudy, fire, castle, hooded, faceless" without copying the show? The rule is [brand-hub/PLAN.md](../brand-hub/PLAN.md) "Show themes evoke, never copy": no logos, wordmarks, artwork or theme music, with show names used descriptively only.

**Hosts: this is the "gaudy".**
- **Alan Cumming (US).** Styled by Sam Spector, who got a 2026 CDG nomination. Over three seasons the looks went from "nice little tartan suits" to "insane capes and weird dresses", in a "creepy glam" register. Hats, gloves, scarves, capes and brooches. Specific looks:
  - "magenta and black tartan suit over a black turtleneck"
  - a "burgundy velvet shirt"
  - a "red velvet suit underneath a … floor-length sleeveless shawl coat in red and black plaid"
  - "plaid opera gloves and matching kilt"

  He calls his persona "a James Bond villain". Sources: [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/the-traitors-alan-cumming-outfits-ranked-1851299855), [Collider](https://collider.com/alan-cumming-the-traitors-season-2/), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/the-traitors-new-season-alan-cummings).
- **Claudia Winkleman (UK).** Styled by Sinead McKeefry. "Punk glamour" built on tartan, oversized knitwear, capes, faux fur, chunky boots and **red fingerless gloves**, the signature that began as an Amazon stopgap. "Moody, gothic, even a little theatrical". Sources: [Hello!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/shopping/926308/claudia-winkleman-the-traitors-outfits-looks-to-shop/), [AOL / stylist interview](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/asked-claudia-winkleman-stylist-traitors-164856000.html).

**What to steer clear of, beyond the logo:**
- The "Murrrder" glitter jumper. Its designer accused NBC merch of copying it ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/news/scottish-designer-says-traitors-design-230303459.html)), so it's someone's design.
- The exact breakfast-letter sentence.
- Any registered or corporate tartan. Registration creates no IP by itself, but restricted setts exist and copyright can apply ([clan.com](https://clan.com/help/tartan-help/tartan-design-rules-regulations), [WP: Scottish Register of Tartans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Register_of_Tartans)). We design our own sett.

**Recommended pair: Cinzel (display) + EB Garamond (body)**, with Cinzel Decorative held back for the title card and Caveat for the slate.
- Load through `next/font/google`, as `frontend/app/layout.tsx` already does.
- Use `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums` on EB Garamond for points. Its default figures are old-style, which jitter in a leaderboard.
- [OFL](https://openfontlicense.org/) lets us bundle and modify the fonts. A font can't be sold on its own, and reserved names can't go on modified versions.

## 3. Palette

**Tartan accents.** Our own sett, in tokens only: ground `cloak-500`, overcheck `oxblood`, a navy `#1C2A44` band (from the interior palette), and thin `gilt` and `bone` lines. That echoes Cumming's red-and-black plaid without being a registered sett.

**Rules the numbers force:**
- Red text is `blood-hi`, never `blood`.
- `ember` and `candle` buttons take `night` text (6.2 and 10.7), never white.
- `ash-dim` never carries copy.
- Fire glow (`text-shadow`) is decoration. Contrast is measured against the flat background, so glow never stands in for it.

## 4. Motion and FX that run in the browser

Already in `frontend/` and `hub/`: three 0.186, @react-three/fiber 9.8, drei 10.7.9, @react-three/postprocessing 3.1.3. Both intros already use Bloom, Vignette and ToneMapping, with `dpr={[1, 1.75]}` (`frontend/components/intro-scene/intro-scene.tsx:86,115`). npm versions below are from `npm view` on 2026-10-02.

| Effect | Approach | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Candle and torch flicker (3D) | 2-4 `pointLight`s, warm `#FF9A3C`, intensity driven by summed sines plus noise in `useFrame`, each light with its own phase. Flame = additive billboard plane with a small noise shader. Bloom at threshold ~0.8 does the glow | Each point light adds per-fragment cost on every lit material. Keep it to 4 or fewer. A baked lightmap is cheaper (CC0 HDRIs below) |
| Candle flicker (2D) | CSS `@keyframes` on `opacity`/`filter` of a radial-gradient overlay, at irregular percentages. References: [antoniandre candle](https://codepen.io/antoniandre/pen/aRPJoM), [CSS flame](https://codepen.io/chris22smith/pen/XXdzzy) | Cheap. Animate `opacity`/`transform` only, never `box-shadow` |
| Embers | drei [`<Sparkles>`](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/staging/sparkles) (count, speed, size, color, noise): one draw call, colour `ember`/`flame`, upward drift. Heavier: [three.quarks](https://www.npmjs.com/package/three.quarks) 0.17.1 MIT, or [three-nebula](https://www.npmjs.com/package/three-nebula) 13.3.0 MIT | Sparkles at 60-150 is fine on phones |
| Smoke and fog | drei [`<Clouds>/<Cloud>`](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/staging/cloud): instanced, "a single, instanced draw call", with a tight `limit`. Plus `scene.fog` | Transparent overdraw is the real phone cost. Keep segments low and keep clouds off full screen |
| Fire-reveal text | CSS: layered `text-shadow` white→yellow→orange→red ([1stWebDesigner](https://1stwebdesigner.com/make-fire-animation-text-effect-css/)). Burn-in: SVG [`feTurbulence`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Element/feTurbulence) + `feDisplacementMap` with `baseFrequency` animated, plus a gradient mask moving up | SVG filters on large text repaint every frame. Run them once on the reveal, not continuously |
| Wax seal stamp ("lock in") | SVG seal in `blood`/`oxblood` with an embossed glyph of our own (a hood, never the show emblem). Keyframes: scale 1.6→0.95→1 with a slight rotate, a drop shadow that tightens, a 1-frame screen shake, `navigator.vibrate(20)` where supported | Pure CSS/SVG |
| Chalk slate writing | Caveat text masked by an SVG stroke-dashoffset path, or a `clip-path: inset()` sweep. An `feTurbulence` grain filter on the letters, and a slate texture behind | Cheap |
| Parchment and scroll | ambientCG paper texture or CSS radial-gradient vignette, `feTurbulence` fractalNoise at ~5% opacity, scroll unroll as `scaleY` on a clip | Cheap |
| Tartan | Stacked `repeating-linear-gradient` at 0° and 90° with transparent stops, plus a `mix-blend-mode: multiply` twill line at 45° ([LogRocket blend modes](https://blog.logrocket.com/advanced-effects-with-css-background-blend-modes-4b750198522a/), [tartan generator pen](https://codepen.io/niallains/pen/ExEOmdJ)). In 3D, render it once to a `CanvasTexture` for cloak linings and banners | No image asset |
| Cloth (cloak drop) | `MeshDistortMaterial` exists in drei 10.7.9 but reads as jelly. Use a vertex shader on a subdivided plane with gravity-weighted sin waves instead, or a pre-baked morph | Shader-only |

**Phone budget**, from [R3F scaling performance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance):
- Keep draw calls low. Each mesh is one.
- Instance repeats: the hooded walkers are one `InstancedMesh`.
- Use `<PerformanceMonitor>` to step down dpr and drop Bloom or Clouds when FPS falls. `<AdaptiveDpr>` is also available.
- Keep the existing `dpr={[1, 1.75]}` clamp, the poster frame, the 8 s patience and the 2D fallback.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` skips the intro and freezes flicker into a static glow.

**Skip.** `lamina` 1.2.2: last published 2025-06-21. Its archived status is **unverified**; either way it isn't needed.

## Design direction

1. **Palette and tokens.** Green-black `night` and `stone` grounds, `cloak` green surfaces, `candle` gold for anything worth points, `blood-hi`/`oxblood` reserved for Traitor. Body text is `parchment` (14.3:1).
2. **Type.** Cinzel for headings and labels, EB Garamond body with tabular lining numbers. Cinzel Decorative only for "Traitors" on the title card and the Faithful/Traitor reveal word. Caveat for chalked names. No blackletter beyond an optional single word.
3. **Gaudy comes from the hosts.** Tartan and gilt, layered on by Cumming's rule: our own red/black/green sett on banners, card edges and the leaderboard runner. Velvet-dark surfaces, gold frames. Faces never shown.
4. **One lock-in gesture.** Every final pick ends in the same wax-seal stamp, with haptics where supported.
5. **The ballot is a slate.** Rank I/II/III by chalking names, the show's own voting mechanic turned into UI.
6. **Reveals are hood drops.** Hooded, faceless card → hood falls → *Faithful.* or *Traitor.* burns in. The murder result is an empty breakfast chair with the candle out.
7. **Fire on a budget.** At most 4 flickering point lights, Sparkles embers, instanced Clouds, Bloom. `PerformanceMonitor` steps quality down, and reduced motion gets a static glow. 2D screens use CSS flicker and one-shot SVG fire, never a continuous filter.
8. **Red cloak as a rare accent.** Green cloaks for the cast. A single red cloak can mark something special (the winner bet, a perfect week), since both shows used red for the Secret Traitor.
9. **Clean-room rules.** Top-down table OK; never the franchise compass emblem (8-point star + crescent hub + moon-phase ring). Ours is a 12-point star with our hood at the hub and plain gilt candle discs at the places. Also no "Murrrder" styling, no breakfast-letter wording, no registered tartan, no show audio. CC0 assets first, CC-BY only with an entry on the Credits page.
